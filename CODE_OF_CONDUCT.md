# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the TotalDrop community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy international engineering community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Communicating clearly, constructively, and respectfully in English across GitHub issues, pull requests, and reviews
- Focusing technical critiques on code, threat models, and evidence rather than individuals
- Respecting contributors across all 35 supported language communities and diverse hardware platforms
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information without explicit permission, or conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers at `hello@totaldrop.app`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, respecting the privacy and security of the reporter.
